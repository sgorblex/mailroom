# Ideas

Exploratory, not scoped, not scheduled, no maturity. Promote to [`TODO.md`](TODO.md) or
[`REQUIREMENTS.md`](REQUIREMENTS.md) when agreed; delete when dropped.

- **Explain a suggestion in plain words** next to the generated rule ("every mail from these three
  senders went to Receipts").
- **Suggestion quality metrics** — how often accepted suggestions are later edited or disabled.
- **Unsubscribe helper** — `List-Unsubscribe` one-click, never automatic.
- **Per-sender remote-content allowlist** for newsletters a user trusts.
- **Filters over several messages at once**, rather than each message alone: duplicates first.
  Within one folder is the easy case (the same `Message-ID`, or the same sender, date and
  body); across folders, and across accounts (the same mail received by several mailboxes), is
  the hard one. Mailroom's filters look at one message at a time today.
